Privacy policy

Effective 28 October 2026. Applies to the PleatView TV app and the PleatView Home Assistant integration, developed and published by Hustlesman.

Summary

PleatView does not collect, sell or share personal data. It has no accounts, analytics, advertising, crash reporting or third-party tracking. The TV app communicates only with your own Home Assistant server and with media addresses your Home Assistant sends to it.

What the TV app stores

On the TV itself, PleatView stores:

  • the address of your Home Assistant server and an access token used to show your dashboards,
  • its settings (dashboards, zoom, kiosk mode and similar),
  • a random device identifier, derived from Android's per-app identifier, so Home Assistant recognises the TV,
  • one-way hashes of the pairing tokens it has issued.

This data stays on the TV. It is excluded from Android backups and device-to-device transfers. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes it.

Network connections

The TV app listens on your local network (port 8765) so a paired Home Assistant can control it, and announces itself on the local network so Home Assistant can find it. Pairing requires a PIN shown on the TV. The app connects to your Home Assistant server and to the media, image and web addresses that Home Assistant asks it to show. It makes no other connections.

Home Assistant integration

The integration runs inside your own Home Assistant. If you choose to, it creates a local-only Home Assistant user for the TV and gives the TV an access token for that user. Removing the integration deletes that user and resets the TV.

App stores

If you install PleatView from an app store, the store's own privacy policy covers the data it collects, such as downloads and crash statistics it gathers itself.

Changes and contact

Changes to this policy are published on this page and in the project's GitHub repository. Questions about privacy: open an issue on GitHub.

Who we are

PleatView is developed and published by Hustlesman. Optional donations made through the website are processed by Stripe and appear as Hustlesman on card statements. The app itself handles no payments.
