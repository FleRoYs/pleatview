Your Home Assistant dashboards, always on your TV.

A lightweight Google TV app with a Home Assistant integration. Keep dashboards on screen around the clock, pop up cameras when someone rings, cast media and send notifications to the big screen.

Get started View on GitHub

What it does

Always-on dashboards

Any dashboard full screen, kept running for days. Recovers from Wi-Fi drops, Home Assistant restarts and browser crashes on its own.

Cast from Home Assistant

The TV becomes a media player: play from the media browser, internet radio, text-to-speech announcements and web pages.

Camera pop-ups

Show a camera's live stream full screen or in a corner over the dashboard, for example when the doorbell rings.

Notifications

Send cards to the TV with notify.send_message, including a camera snapshot. They can also appear over other apps.

Made for the remote

Switch dashboards with channel up/down, close pop-ups with Back, and use the menu without a keyboard.

Private and local

No cloud, no accounts, no tracking. The TV talks only to your own Home Assistant on your network.

Get started

  1. Install the TV app.On the TV, install the free Downloader app from the store, open it and enter pleatview.hustlesman.com/apk. Android asks once to allow the install. No computer needed.
  2. Add the integration.Open PleatView in HACS on your Home Assistant with the button below, install it, and restart Home Assistant. Open in HACS
  3. Pair.Home Assistant discovers the TV. Select Add and enter the PIN shown on the TV; your dashboard appears a few seconds later. Set up manually

Prefer adb, or want other sign-in options? See the install guide. Store versions are on the roadmap.
